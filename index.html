One Division, Three Answers
26 ÷ 6 every time. The arithmetic never changes.
5
26 kids, vans hold 6. Two kids still need a seat, so you round up.

26 cookies, cut the last two up. Now the leftover becomes a fraction.
All three are correct. The question picks the answer, not the division.

Angles That Add Up

What the Letter Is Not
Four wrong readings that cause almost every mistake in this mission.
3n
Three lots of n. Not thirty-something, and not “three next to n.”
n
A number nobody has named yet. Not an abbreviation for a word.
=
A claim that both sides weigh the same. Not “the answer comes next.”
2n ≠ n²
Doubling and squaring agree only at 0 and 2. Test them at 5.
Every equation he solves gets checked by substitution. That habit makes the answer key optional.

The error journal
One spiral notebook. Every wrong answer gets three lines and then it's over — no re-doing the whole page.
Sample entry
Problem: 47 × 32
I got: 1424   Answer: 1504
Why: I forgot the 7×30 room.
The sentence is the whole point. “I was careless” doesn't count — it has to name the step.
