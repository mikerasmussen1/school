When the Average Lies

One extreme value. Watch carefully which measures move and which do not.

Range of 5, 5, 5, 25
Mode of 5, 5, 5, 25
◆
Core
graded · show your working
1.  Four people earn 10 each, a fifth earns 60 — mean (100 ÷ 5)
2.  Those same five people — median
3.  Those same five people — range
