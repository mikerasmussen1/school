The shaded amount is identical. All that changed is how many pieces the bar was cut into — and cutting each piece into three makes three times as many pieces, three times as many of them shaded.

That is why multiplying top and bottom by 3 is allowed: 1/2 = 3/6. You are renaming the amount, not changing it.

When the Bottoms Match

Add the tops. The bottom is the unit, and it stays put.
NAME ___________
DATE ___________
✦
Warm-Up
1/8 + 3/8
3/8 + 2/8
5/6 − 1/6
2/5 + 2/5
7/10 − 3/10
3/4 + 1/4
The denominator names the unit
3/8 + 2/8 = 5/8
Three eighths plus two eighths is five eighths, the same way three apples plus two apples is five apples. The word “eighths” is the unit, and it does not change just because you added some more of them.

This is exactly why you cannot add 1/2 to 1/3 directly. Halves and thirds are different units, and there is no single word for the answer until you make them the same.

Three ways to compare, fastest first
Against a half
3/8 is under a half, 5/9 is over. Done — no arithmetic needed.
Same top
3/5 beats 3/8, because fifths are bigger pieces than eighths.
Common denominator
Always works, slowest. Save it for when the first two do not settle it.
A half is the useful landmark because you can see it at a glance: double the top and compare it to the bottom.

Mission 05 · Week 1 · Thursday · Lesson 1.4

Why 2/5 Is Wrong

The most common fraction error in the world, taken apart with a diagram.
NAME ___________
DATE ___________
✦
Warm-Up
Estimate: more or less than 1?
1/2 + 1/3
3/4 + 1/2
1/4 + 1/3
5/8 + 1/2
1/5 + 1/4
2/3 + 2/3
1/2 + 1/3 · the answer must be bigger than a half
1/2
2/5 — the wrong answer, shown
5/6 — the right answer
You started with a half and added something to it. The answer has to be more than a half.

But 2/5 is less than a half. So it is wrong before you check any arithmetic — and that check takes one second.

Adding the bottoms makes the pieces smaller, which is the opposite of what adding should do.
