How the year is built

Nine unit studies, four weeks each: 36 weeks, 180 days. Every week runs the same five-day rhythm, and each day is one screen your child can start alone. Monday reading, Tuesday grammar, Wednesday spelling, Thursday handwriting, Friday speaking and review.

Grammar, spelling and reading questions grade themselves instantly with an explanation. Thursday's handwritten page is photographed and read by the model. Friday's speaking task is deliberately NOT machine-graded: there is no honest way to score a child reporting aloud or building on someone's idea in a discussion, so the page gives the task and the rubric and your child marks it done with whoever they did it with. That is the one place in the week that genuinely needs another person.
