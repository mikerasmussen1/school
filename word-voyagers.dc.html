How the days are shaped

Nine unit studies, four weeks each: 36 weeks, 180 days. Every week runs the same five-day rhythm, and each day is one screen your child can start alone. Monday reading, Tuesday grammar, Wednesday spelling, Thursday handwriting, Friday speaking and review.

Grammar, spelling and reading questions grade themselves instantly with an explanation. Thursday's handwritten page is photographed and read by the model. Friday's speaking task is deliberately NOT machine-graded: there is no honest way to score a child reporting aloud or building on someone's idea in a discussion, so the page gives the task and the rubric and your child marks it done with whoever they did it with. That is the one place in the week that genuinely needs another person.

Scheduled breaks

There is no fixed last day — the year runs its 180 school days and ends when they are used up. Break days are not school days, so adding a break pushes the end date later rather than costing a lesson. To change these, edit BREAKS at the top of curriculum/la-calendar.js.

An unplanned week off costs nothing here — there is no pace to fall off. Those days simply wait. Add them above if you want them off the calendar, or leave them; either way the year ends when the 180 days are done.

Catching up and working ahead

Nothing is locked anywhere in this curriculum. Every day and every step is open, in any order, and a drill that comes in under 80% still ticks its step off. What the app does instead is tell him: a day with unfinished work behind it opens with a warning naming that day and a button back to it, and a short score offers another round rather than demanding one. He can also work as far ahead as he likes.

The trade is deliberate and yours to reverse: he cannot be walled out of school by a bad morning, and he also cannot be stopped from skipping something. The two lists below are how you see what actually happened.

Excuse a day

If a day is genuinely never going to be made up — illness, a family week, or you started partway through the year — excuse it here. An excused day counts as finished for ordering only, so the rest of the year opens. Without this, one lost week would wall your child out of everything after it, and a rule with no release valve is a trap rather than a rule.
